# Tech Lead / Lead Programmer Interview Preparation

## 1. How would you describe your leadership style?

I lead through technical ownership, clear communication, and partnership with the people doing the work. I am comfortable making and explaining architecture decisions, but I also value early input from developers and stakeholders. My goal is to create clarity around the problem, the tradeoffs, and the next safe step forward.

## 2. Tell me about a system you led architecturally.

I am the sole architect and lead developer of NASA Earthdata Pub Overview and Forms, used by all 12 NASA DAACs and their Data Producers. I have led the application design and modernization decisions, including dashboard work, API integration, authentication, user workflows, testing, and support. The role requires both technical depth and careful coordination with the people affected by changes.

## 3. Give an example of delivering work with other developers.

I delivered the ORNL DAAC Ingest Dashboard with a three-developer team. My contribution included helping shape an architecture intended to interface with Earthdata Pub for quality assurance and fuller automation. I focus on defining integration points, making ownership clear, and keeping the work connected to the operational need.

## 4. How do you handle a disagreement on a technical direction?

I bring the discussion back to the problem, constraints, and evidence. I want the options and tradeoffs to be visible—delivery speed, operational risk, maintainability, security, and user impact. Once a decision is made, I document the reasoning enough that the team can move forward together and revisit it if conditions change.

## 5. How do you help a team improve quality and delivery?

I use practices that make good work repeatable: focused automated tests, CI/CD, codebase modernization, clear interfaces, and practical documentation. I have implemented automated unit and end-to-end tests to improve deployment confidence, and I have automated workflows that reduced manual processing time by 80%. These practices support both quality and developer effectiveness.

## Questions to ask the interviewer

- What technical and people leadership responsibilities are expected of this lead?
- How are architecture decisions made and recorded?
- What delivery or reliability challenge would be most important to improve first?
