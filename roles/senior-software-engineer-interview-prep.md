# Senior Software Engineer Interview Preparation

## 1. What makes you a senior engineer?

For me, seniority is owning outcomes beyond a single feature: understanding the system and its users, making sound technical decisions, improving reliability, and helping the team deliver sustainably. My current role includes end-to-end architecture ownership for NASA Earthdata Pub applications used across all 12 NASA DAACs and their Data Producers.

## 2. Describe a complex system you modernized or evolved.

At NASA / ORNL DAAC, I have modernized and maintained the web platform while supporting migration to NASA's cloud-hosted Earthdata environment. I also modernized sites and scripts from Perl to Python and repurposed NASA's Cumulus Dashboard for Earthdata Pub. I approach modernization incrementally: preserve essential behavior, identify high-value seams, add confidence through testing, and steadily lower maintenance cost.

## 3. How do you make architecture decisions?

I begin with requirements, users, data flows, operational constraints, and the cost of future change. I make the tradeoffs explicit and aim for a solution the team can understand and support. As sole architect and lead developer of Earthdata Pub applications, I have had to balance user workflows, API integration, data handling, testing, and long-term evolution rather than optimizing one layer in isolation.

## 4. How do you improve reliability?

I combine design clarity with feedback loops: clear interfaces, automated unit and end-to-end tests, CI/CD practices, and attention to recurring operational work. I have implemented automated testing to improve deployment confidence and reduce regressions, and I have automated processing workflows that reduced manual work by 80%.

## 5. How do you work with stakeholders who are not engineers?

I translate technical choices into the outcome they care about—whether that is faster work, fewer errors, clearer reporting, or a more dependable service. I have partnered directly with research teams, doctors, agency users, publishing teams, and technical colleagues. Listening carefully to the real workflow has consistently led to better software decisions.

## Questions to ask the interviewer

- What are the most consequential systems or technical decisions this senior engineer will influence?
- How does the organization balance feature delivery, reliability, and modernization?
- What would success in the first year look like for this role?
